Soju-Mojito Recipe

Delightful Spring Drink

What is Soju-Mojito?

A Soju Mojito is a Soju-based drink that combines the crisp flavors of fresh mint, lime, syrup and soda water. It’s an easy-to-make drink that’s perfect for warm weather or when you want a light, zesty cocktail with a little extra kick.

Prep time: 1 min | Mixing time: 1 mins

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Take 8–12 fresh mint leaves in one hand and gently smack them with the other to release their aroma. Drop them into a highball glass and add ¾ oz cane sugar syrup.
  2. Pour in 1 oz of fresh lime juice.
  3. Add 2 oz of Soju to the glass.
  4. Fill the glass three-quarters full with crushed ice.
  5. Use a bar spoon or long stirrer to churn and mix the ingredients thoroughly.
  6. Insert a straw and top with more crushed ice, creating a mound on top.
  7. Pour in 1–2 oz of soda water to fill the glass.
  8. Garnish with a lime slice and a sprig of mint. Serve immediately.
