Jasmine Tea Recipe

Delightful Spring Drink

What is Jasmine Tea?

Jasmine tea is a caffeinated, scented tea usually made from combining green tea leaves with jasmine petals. It has a delicate flavor and a full, floral aroma from the jasmine flowers. Traditionally, jasmine tea was scented by placing trays of fresh jasmine flowers below a woven tray of tea leaves in a warm room. Now, it’s common for the tea and flowers to be combined as the tea gets processed.

Prep time: 1 min | Mixing time: 1 mins

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Boil water. Using an electric kettle with a temperature setting to boil water for tea makes it easy to get the water temperature just right. Boil more than needed since you want extra to warm up the teapot.
  2. Warm up the teapot by pouring some hot water into the teapot and swirling it around a bit. Discard the water.
  3. Put jasmine tea into the teapot and add hot water. Cover the teapot and steep.
  4. Strain jasmine tea and pour hot tea into a teacup.
