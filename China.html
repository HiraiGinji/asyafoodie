China Blue By Jereme Leung

China Blue by Jereme Leung, located within the Conrad Manila, brings a sophisticated and modern interpretation of traditional Chinese cuisine to the Philippines. Spearheaded by the internationally acclaimed Master Chef Jereme Leung, the restaurant offers a dining experience that seamlessly blends authentic flavors with contemporary culinary techniques. Since its establishment, China Blue has become a prominent destination for gourmands seeking refined Chinese cooking, boasting a stylish ambiance characterized by glittering blue chandeliers and panoramic views of Manila Bay, creating an elegant yet welcoming atmosphere for its patrons.

Chef Jereme Leung's vision for China Blue was consciously tailored for the Manila market, thoughtfully incorporating local nuances into his expertly crafted menu. The restaurant quickly gained recognition for its exceptional dim sum and barbecued specialties, establishing itself as a leader in contemporary Chinese gastronomy in the country. Signature creations like the much-imitated steamed truffle mushroom bun and the succulent honey-glazed Matsusaka pork neck have garnered widespread acclaim, highlighting the innovative spirit and culinary artistry that define the China Blue by Jereme Leung experience.

Sea Bass

Presented on a pristine white plate, the expertly steamed sea bass is bathed in a light, fragrant soy-based sauce, its subtle umami enhancing the inherent flavors of the sea. Adorning the fish are vibrant sprigs of fresh herbs, likely including cilantro and perhaps delicate strands of ginger or scallion, adding a refreshing aromatic lift to the dish. The gentle steaming process ensures the fish remains incredibly moist and tender, offering a melt-in-your-mouth experience.

Chicken Soup

Served in an elegant, pristine white bowl, the consommé is remarkably clear, hinting at the hours of gentle simmering that have melded the essence of the finest chicken with carefully selected ingredients. Within the luminous broth, you'll discover tender pieces of chicken, likely accompanied by delicate additions such as fragrant mushrooms, verdant baby bok choy, and the subtle sweetness of goji berries.

Dim sum

Presented in a traditional bamboo steamer, these three perfectly formed buns boast a unique, glossy charcoal-black exterior, giving them an air of contemporary elegance.  Adorning each bun are delicate, hand-painted gold stripes, adding a touch of opulence and artistry. Nestled within their soft, yielding exterior is a warm,流沙 (liú shā) or "flowing sand" salted egg yolk custard filling. This signature filling is rich, slightly sweet, and possesses a wonderfully smooth, molten texture that oozes out with each bite, creating a delightful contrast with the fluffy bun.
