Turon Recipe

Filipinos' favourite Snack!

What is Turon?

Turon is a type of Filipino snack. It is also called banana lumpia due to its similarity to the popular Filipino lumpia. A typical Filipino meryenda and dessert, Turon has gained popularity throughout the years. This sweet snack can usually be seen on the streets being peddled along with bananacue or even vegetable lumpia.

Prep time: 10 mins | Cook time: 12 mins

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Roll each banana slice in sugar until evenly coated.
  2. Place the sugar-coated banana on a lumpia wrapper and add a slice of ripe jackfruit on top.
  3. Fold and seal the wrapper tightly, using a bit of water to lock the edges.
  4. Heat 2 cups of cooking oil in a pan and sprinkle in some sugar. Wait until the sugar caramelizes and floats.
  5. Carefully add the wrapped bananas to the pan and fry until golden brown, allowing the caramelized sugar to stick to the wrapper.
